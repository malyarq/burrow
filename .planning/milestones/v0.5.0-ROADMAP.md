# Milestone v0.5.0: Experience Reinvention And Brand Reset

**Status:** ✅ SHIPPED 2026-04-20
**Phases:** 19-27
**Total Plans:** 36

## Overview

`v0.5.0` turned FMCL from an increasingly inconsistent launcher into a deliberate redesign-quality release. The milestone first stabilized shell geometry and CTA ownership, then reset brand and fallback rules, rebuilt dense modpack information architecture, made theme and locale state truthful, productized degraded and crash states, and closed on deterministic proof plus release-truth sync.

The shipped milestone also includes its own proof recovery. Phases 25-27 did not reopen product scope; they restored requirement-backed verification artifacts, closed Nyquist gaps, retired stale residual debt language, and brought the milestone to a clean audit pass before archive.

## Milestone Summary

**Decimal Phases:**

- None

**Key Decisions:**

- Began the redesign by hardening shell geometry and CTA ownership first, so later surface changes inherited stable layout truth instead of route-local hacks.
- Treated branding, logo usage, and missing-art fallbacks as shared product seams rather than decorative route-level decisions.
- Rebuilt dense modpack flows around one runtime-summary and action-ownership truth instead of allowing separate browse, detail, create, and edit interpretations.
- Kept closeout proof on the existing manual seam, then added deterministic screenshot coverage instead of introducing a second verification harness.
- Recovered missing audit evidence through explicit proof-recovery phases instead of pretending the earlier phases had already shipped with complete verification artifacts.

**Issues Resolved:**

- Content rendering under the custom title bar, footer-style overlap, and competing primary actions on major launcher routes.
- Arbitrary brand drift, repetitive or irritating logo use, and broken missing-art fallback behavior.
- Dense modpack browser and details surfaces with ambiguous hierarchy, conflicting actions, and untruthful create/edit summaries.
- Weak dark/light state fidelity, inconsistent accent propagation, locale drift, and raw placeholder leakage in shipped UI.
- Ambiguous empty, degraded, and fatal-error states, plus release-truth and audit-trail drift at milestone closeout.

**Issues Deferred:**

- No archive-blocking issues remain. Future work is intentionally left to fresh milestone planning rather than carried as active `v0.5.0` debt.

**Technical Debt Incurred:**

- None active. Historical proof-story caveats were retired into archival context before the final audit pass.
